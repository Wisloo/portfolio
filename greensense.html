What GreenSense does

Know your real costs

Log every purchase — seeds, soil, fertilizer, labor — and GreenSense calculates the actual cost per crop. No more guessing.

See what sells

Record sales as they happen. Over time, GreenSense shows you which crops bring in the most revenue and which ones cost you money.

Catch problems early

Get a heads-up when spending spikes, margins shrink, or a crop isn't pulling its weight — before it becomes a bigger issue.

Simple reports

Clean charts and summaries you can actually read. Weekly and monthly breakdowns, no spreadsheets required.
